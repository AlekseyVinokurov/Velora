# Velora features

This catalog describes capabilities present in Velora. For changes in a particular version, see [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/AlekseyVinokurov/Velora/releases); ideas and bug reports belong in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/AlekseyVinokurov/Velora/issues).

## Music

Music is the main focus of Velora.

- **Library and playlists:** browse saved music, organize tracks and work with playlists.
- **Music search:** find tracks through the sources available in the app.
- **Player and queue:** control playback, navigate between tracks and manage their order.
- **Background playback:** continue listening while using other apps or with the screen off.
- **Sound settings:** equalizer and audio effects.
- **Local music and storage:** existing import, cache and download capabilities.

Music source availability can vary. Finding a track in search does not necessarily mean that a playable stream is available.

## Telegram

- Private chats, groups and channels.
- Voice and video calls, files and other Telegram media.
- Multiple accounts, stories and notifications.

## Interface and personalization

- Themes, colors and appearance settings.
- Icon options, wallpapers and additional chat preferences.

## Additional and experimental features

Velora also includes optional functions beyond the standard Telegram experience:

- **Ghost mode:** additional controls related to activity and read-status behavior.
- **Message history:** local handling of edits and deletions for messages already available to the client.
- **Extended preferences:** other client behavior and privacy settings.

These features are evolving. Their behavior depends on the app version and Telegram's capabilities; they should not be treated as guarantees of invisibility or recovery of unavailable messages.
