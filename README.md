# Velora

**Music and Telegram in one app.**

Velora is an independent Telegram client for Android with a focus on music. It combines a full-featured messenger with a music library, player and additional ways to customize the experience.

## About the project

Velora is my personal project, developed primarily for my own use and shared with anyone who finds it interesting.

I want to bring a full-featured Telegram client and a convenient music experience together, while exploring new ideas, settings and features.

Beyond the familiar Telegram experience, Velora includes additional tools, extended settings and experimental features. Some are still evolving — there's always something new to explore and try.

**Velora is open source.** You're welcome to download the app for free, explore the code, modify it and contribute improvements under its license.

## Features

- **Music:** library, track search, playlists, playback queue, background playback and audio settings.
- **Telegram:** chats, groups, channels, calls, file sharing and multiple accounts.
- **Personalization:** themes, colors, icons and interface settings.
- **More to explore:** additional controls and experimental features.

See the [feature catalog](docs/FEATURES.md) for a closer look at what's available.

## Download

Download available Android builds from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/AlekseyVinokurov/Velora/releases). Installed stable builds also include an in-app update checker.

## Development

Music is the main direction for Velora: more music sources, better discovery and recommendations, and ongoing improvements to the player and library. New capabilities are documented as they become available.

You can share ideas and report problems in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/AlekseyVinokurov/Velora/issues). Changes in each published version are listed in [Releases](https://github.com/AlekseyVinokurov/Velora/releases).
